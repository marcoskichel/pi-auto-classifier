# TLDR

The reply must follow these rules.

- Lead with the answer or result in the first sentence. Everything else is secondary.
- No preamble of any kind: no "Sure", no "Great question", no restating the task.
- No trivia, no filler, no background the user did not ask for.
- No caveats or hedging unless the risk is real and actionable.
- No closing summary that repeats what was already said.
- Prefer short lists over prose paragraphs. Prefer one line over a list.
- If a sentence can be deleted without losing meaning, delete it. Most can.

## Judging

Be strict. Fail for: any preamble, a buried answer, filler sentences, trivia,
restating the question, hedging, or a redundant closing summary. Pass only
replies that are answer-first and tight throughout.
